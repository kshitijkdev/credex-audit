Good reflection example:

```md
# Reflection

## What Went Well

- User interviews significantly improved the audit logic
- The deterministic audit engine became more trustworthy than AI-generated recommendations
- Shipping quickly with Next.js + Vercel reduced deployment overhead

---

## What Went Wrong

I initially underestimated how long the entrepreneurial files would take.

I also discovered that users care less about subscription count and more about workflow overlap.

One interviewee explicitly said:
"I don't think any such tool is needed — one subscription can do it all."

That challenged my assumption that users actively optimize AI spend.

---

## Where AI Helped

AI accelerated:
- UI scaffolding
- TypeScript debugging
- component generation
- architecture planning

---

## Where AI Was Wrong

AI initially suggested overly generic GTM ideas:
- "SEO"
- "content marketing"

Those ideas were too broad and not realistic for an early-stage product.

I replaced them with specific founder communities and distribution channels.
