## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no trial period. The audit is free because it's a lead-generation tool for Credex — we're honest about that. If your savings are significant, we'll tell you about Credex credits. If not, we'll tell you you're spending well.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: We store your tool selections and spend figures (no company name or email) to generate your shareable link. We never sell your data. Email is only collected after you've seen your results — never before.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: Every number traces to an official vendor pricing page, cited with a verification date. The audit logic is rules-based — not AI guessing — so the math is transparent and auditable. A finance person should be able to read every recommendation and agree with the reasoning.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that overforecast or pivoted. The discount is real. This tool surfaces whether you'd benefit from credits based on your actual spend.

**Q: What if my spend is already optimized?**
A: We'll tell you. We don't manufacture savings opportunities. If you're spending well, you'll see "You're spending well ✓" and an option to get notified when new optimizations apply to your stack.
